Gapline Schema

The PostgreSQL schema behind a NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 self-assessment. A versioned control library sits on one side and each customer's assessment on the other. These drawings show how the two connect and what changes once a senior official signs.

Still editable after signing: ssp_narrative, the living SSP, and poam_item, so remediation can be tracked to close-out. Not a guard against the owner: every rule here is a trigger, and a role that owns the tables can disable triggers, so the app has to connect as a non-owner role.

Scoring

requirement_status starts from the library rather than from the results, so a requirement stays unreviewed until every one of its objectives has a human result, and it is met only when all of them are met or not applicable. AI and rule results are kept beside the human one and never score. The SPRS figure itself (110 minus deductions, as low as −203) is left to application code, which isn't written yet. The schema notes the rule it must follow: no score while any requirement is unreviewed or 3.12.4 is not met.
